CHECK YOUR UNDERSTANDING

Mini Learning Lab

Four quick questions. Four core concepts.

Try these questions to check your understanding. Select an answer and check your result.

01. Which statement best describes an algorithm?

02. Which data structure normally follows FIFO?

03. Which component performs arithmetic and logical operations?

04. If P is True and Q is False, what is P AND Q?

If a question is difficult, revisit the relevant exhibit. The goal is to understand concepts, not simply memorise answers.
