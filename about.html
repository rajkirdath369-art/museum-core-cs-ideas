About the Project

An educational website about foundational Computer Science.

The Museum of Core CS Ideas introduces foundational concepts through short explanations, examples, diagrams, and learning resources.

Project objectives

  • Explain core Computer Science concepts in clear language.
  • Organize information into a simple, navigable website.
  • Connect theory to real-world applications.
  • Encourage independent learning and revision.
