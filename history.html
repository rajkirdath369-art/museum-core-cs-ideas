THE TIMELINE

History of Computing

From mechanical calculation to the connected world.

Modern computing grew from discoveries in mathematics, engineering, electronics, and information science. Its history is a collection of ideas and inventions that influenced one another.

EARLY HISTORY

Mechanical calculating devices

Mechanical tools helped people perform arithmetic before electronic computers existed.

1830s

Analytical Engine concept

Charles Babbage proposed a general-purpose mechanical computing machine with ideas associated with programmability.

1930s–1940s

Foundations of modern computing

Theoretical work on computation and the development of electronic machines helped establish modern Computer Science.

1940s–1950s

Stored-program computers

The stored-program approach made instructions represented in memory a major foundation of general-purpose computing.

1970s onward

Microprocessors and personal computing

Greater integration made computers smaller, more accessible, and more widely used.

1990s onward

The connected digital world

The web and internet services transformed communication, education, business, and access to information.

TODAY

Cloud computing and AI

Modern systems support large-scale data processing, connected services, scientific research, and AI applications.

Key takeaway: Understanding the past helps explain why modern computing systems are designed the way they are.
